# VedaMilan AI — Marketing Product Brief

**Audience:** Marketing, growth, content, partnerships  
**Purpose:** Explain what the product is, what it does, how to talk about it, and what **not** to claim.  
**Last updated:** July 2026

---

## 1. One-line pitch

**VedaMilan AI** is an international **Vedic Relationship Intelligence** platform: generate a real Kundli, discover explainable matches, deepen compatibility with Guna Milan and values alignment, learn through **Graha Katha**, consult **Virtual AI Astrologers**, and reflect with **Rishi Sage** — with interpretive guidance, not fortune-telling guarantees.

---

## 2. What is VedaMilan? (Plain English)

VedaMilan is **not** only a matrimony app and **not** only an astrology calculator.

It is one product with **three workspaces** users can switch between:

| Workspace (UI name) | What it is                                              | Who it helps                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Astrology**       | Birth chart, dashas, yogas, learning, AI chart guidance | People who want to understand their Kundli and life themes            |
| **Matrimony**       | Matches, search, connections, chat, deep compatibility  | People seeking marriage / serious partnership with Vedic + values fit |
| **Rishi Sage**      | AI wisdom guides inspired by Vedic sages                | People seeking reflective counsel (not prophecy)                      |

**Core brand idea:** Help people **understand compatibility and themselves** — then build meaningful connections with clarity and calm.

**Taglines already in product:**

- Brand: _AI Powered Vedic Relationship Intelligence Platform_
- Marketing: _Vedic matchmaking with relationship intelligence_
- Hero: _Understand compatibility. Build meaningful connections._

---

## 3. The golden rule for all messaging

> **Our astrology engines calculate. AI only explains.**

- Planetary positions come from **Swiss Ephemeris** + Vedic rule engines (real math).
- AI **must not** invent planet placements, fake scripture quotes, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Always frame results as **traditional / interpretive guidance**, never as scientific fact or destiny guarantees.

**Safe disclaimer (use often):**  
_“This is a traditional Vedic astrological interpretation and should not be considered a guarantee or factual prediction.”_

## 11. Messaging do’s and don’ts

### Do

- Say **relationship intelligence**, **explainable compatibility**, **Vedic learning**, **reflective guidance**
- Emphasize **calm**, **premium**, **international**, **privacy of birth data**
- Use product names exactly: **Graha Katha**, **Rishi Sage**, **AI Guru**, **Situational quiz**, **Your Connection**
- Invite curiosity: “Understand why” / “See it in your chart”

### Don’t

- Promise marriage outcomes, pregnancy, job success, medical cures
- Use fear (“dosha will ruin you”) or panic remedy upsells
- Call AI “a real rishi” or “divine oracle”
- Present celebrity demos as real members
- Claim scientific proof of astrology

## 13. Quick FAQ for marketers

**Q: Is this a dating app?**  
A: No. It’s serious matrimony + Vedic relationship intelligence, with gated messaging.

**Q: Is the astrology “real”?**  
A: Calculations use classical Vedic methods and ephemeris data. Interpretations are traditional/AI-assisted explanations — not guarantees.

**Q: What’s our unique feature?**  
A: Lead with **explainable compatibility + Graha Katha + three modes (Astrology / Matrimony / Rishi Sage)** in one product.

**Q: Can we say “AI predicts your future”?**  
A: No. Say “AI explains your chart and compatibility using calculated data.”

**Q: What’s Graha Katha?**  
A: An interactive library of the 9 Vedic planets (Grahas) — mythology, meaning, 12 houses, remedies, and a link to the user’s own Kundli placement.

**Q: What’s Rishi Sage?**  
A: A separate wisdom mode with AI guides inspired by Vedic sages for reflective questions — education/discourse, not fortune-telling.
